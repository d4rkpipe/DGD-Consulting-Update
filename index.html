01 · About

A young firm with the depth of seasoned ground.

DGD Consulting is a modern company providing consulting and services in the field of geological exploration and mining.

The company's activities are aimed at conducting comprehensive geological, geochemical and geophysical surveys, surface exploration and drilling operations in order to identify promising areas and assess their potential.

In its activities, it combines knowledge and experience, modern technologies and international standards.

The company operates based on global industry requirements, with a deep understanding of local geological and territorial features, and aims to provide customers with long-term, reliable and innovative solutions.

03 · Loyiha yo'l xaritasi

Loyiha yo'l xaritasi

Konsept tug'ilishidan kon qurilishi boshlangunga qadar — har bir bosqichda qaysi ishlar bajariladi va qanday natija beriladi.

1

Desktop Study

Ma'lumotlar tahlili · Ofis bosqichi

Yangi loyiha yoki maydon bo'yicha mavjud barcha ma'lumotlar — geologik xaritalar, ilgari o'tkazilgan tadqiqotlar, davlat arxivlari, ilmiy nashrlar — to'planib chuqur tahlil qilinadi.

  • Davlat geologik fondidan ma'lumotlar so'rash
  • Mavjud xaritalar, hisobotlar va burg'ilash ma'lumotlarini ko'rib chiqish
  • Regional tektonik va metallogeniya tahlili
  • GIS yordamida anomaliya zonalarini ajratish
  • Raqobatchi va qo'shni loyihalar tahlili

2

Targeting

Maqsad hududlarni aniqlash · Prioritetlashtirish

Desktop study xulosalari asosida eng istiqbolli maqsad zonalar — targetlar — belgilanadi. Bu bosqichda dastlabki dala tekshiruvlari, geokimyoviy namunalash va geofizik profiller o'tkaziladi.

  • Rekonn geokimyoviy namunalash (stream sediment, float, chip)
  • Geofizik profillash: magnit, gravimetrik
  • Yer usti strukturaviy kuzatuvi
  • Anomaliyalarni ranjirlash va eng muhim targetlarni tanlash
  • Sahaga chiqish logistikasi va xavfsizlik rejasi
Natija

3

Exploration

Qidiruv · Burg'ilash · Namunalash

Targetlangan hududlarda tizimli qidiruv ishlari olib boriladi. Detallanish o'sib boradi: kuzatuvdan burg'ilashgacha. Bu bosqich loyihaning texnik asosini quradi.

  • Detallanmagan geologik kartalashtirish (1:5000 – 1:1000)
  • Sistemali geokimyoviy namunalash: trench, chip, channel
  • IP, magnit va boshqa geofizik surovlar
  • Dastlabki scout burg'ilash (auger, RC yoki diamond)
  • Infill burg'ilash: ma'lumotlar zichligi oshirish
  • Yadro namunalarini assay laboratoriyaga yuborish
  • 3D geologik model qurish
Natija
Minerallashtirish geometriyasi, sifat parametrlari va MRE uchun ma'lumotlar bazasi
Burg'ilash Core logging 3D model
4

Mineral Resource Estimate (MRE)

JORC / NI 43-101 · Resurs baholash

To'plangan ma'lumotlar JORC yoki NI 43-101 xalqaro standarti asosida mustaqil mutaxassis (Competent Person) tomonidan tahlil qilinadi. Kon hajmi, grad va ishonchlilik toifasi e'lon qilinadi.

  • Ma'lumotlar sifatini tekshirish: QA/QC, twin holes
  • Statistik tahlil: variogramma, geostatistika
  • Blok model va kriging interpolatsiyasi
  • Tonnaj-grad hisob: Inferred → Indicated → Measured
  • Competent Person tomonidan mustaqil tekshiruv
  • Rasmiy MRE hisoboti nashr etish
Natija
Tasdiqlangan mineral resurs: toifa, tonnaj va grad bo'yicha
JORC NI 43-101 Geostatistika
5

Scoping Study

Dastlabki texnik-iqtisodiy baholash

MRE asosida loyihaning umumiy texnik-iqtisodiy maqsadga muvofiqligi tekshiriladi. Aniqlik darajasi past (±35–40%), lekin loyiha PFS'ga o'tishga arziydi-mi degan asosiy savolga javob beradi.

  • Qazib olish usuli va kon hajmini taxminiy aniqlash
  • Boyitish texnologiyasi variantlarini ko'rib chiqish
  • Infratuzilma, suv, energiya ehtiyojlarini baholash
  • Capital va operatsion xarajatlarni taxminan hisoblash
  • Ekologik va ijtimoiy risklarni dastlabki baholash
  • NPV, IRR va payback period hisob (±40% aniqlik)

02 · Services

Six disciplines, one continuous workflow — from outcrop to MRE.

Each service is delivered to international reporting standards, paired with full chain-of-custody data and a final document that holds up under audit.

01

Geological consulting services

Strategic advisory across project lifecycle — from concept screening and concession due diligence to data-room reviews and independent technical opinion.

02

Preparation of comprehensive projects

End-to-end exploration project files — geological models, drilling layouts, sampling protocols, budgets and timelines — packaged for permitting and investor review.

Efficient geological exploration works

Field execution and supervision of trenching, mapping, and drilling programs — optimized for cost-per-discovery and accelerated decision points.

MRE report preparation

Mineral Resource Estimation reports prepared to JORC / CIM standards — block modeling, classification, sensitivity analysis and competent-person sign-off.
